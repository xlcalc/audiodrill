### 1. What this tool is for
With Audiodrill, you can build your reading, listening and speaking skills in foreign languages. Practice pronunciation, listening comprehension and vocabulary, using real-life videos, audio and texts.


### 2. Try these sample activities

 * tasks based on the series [<i>The Flatmates</i>](/?t=//en/a2/flatmates/bbc-flatmates-index.txt), [~English at Work~](/?t=//en/a2/eaw/eaw-index.txt), [~VoA Learning English~](/?t=//en/voa.txt)
 * [common irregular English verbs](/irrverbs)
 * [mini-test on articles](/?t=//en/grammar/articles1.txt)
 * [pronunciation tips and tongue twisters](/?t=//en/pronun/pronun-index.txt)
 * [tasks for Mandarin Chinese learners](/?t=//zh/zh-index.txt)


### 3. Make learning interactive

 * 🎧 Click a word to hear it. In Chrome or Edge, you can also play or shadow (play and repeat) a sentence or the full text. To start, open the playback controls (via ~🗣 Read lines~ in the {{arrow:topMenuBtn y-shift=16|system menu}} or |_Ctrl_| + click on a sentence), choose a mode from the dropdown, and press ▷.
 * 🔍 Select a text to hear it, look it up, translate and add to vocabulary.
 * 🔁 Loop audio/video fragments at custom speed:

 * 📚 Dictate or type in [words and phrases](/words) to look up and hear them in context.

 * 🗣️ Play [voice games](/?game=new) (Chrome/Edge desktop).
 * 📖 Try out other ways of [shadow reading](?t=/tasklist/shadowing.txt).
